Salon Management

Modern salon & spa booking with a customer-facing portal

Multi-branch

Run several locations from the same Odoo database. Each branch has its own opening hours, services, stylists and reporting.

Modern portal

A five-step booking wizard with live availability, branch and stylist browsing, and a secure appointment-management page.

Guest or signed-in

Anyone can book with just an email and phone, and is invited to create a free portal account afterwards to manage the booking.

Live Chair Dashboard

See every chair's status in real time — available, in service, or booked later. Built as a custom OWL component.

Customer Portal

Five-step booking wizard, guest-friendly, and a secure page to view or cancel appointments at any time.

Stylists

Assign a dedicated stylist to each chair, list the services they perform, and show their bio on the public site.

Back-office Workflow

Draft → Confirmed → In progress → Done, with Cancel and No-show as alternative end states. Every transition is logged in the chatter and a transactional email is sent.

Branches & Services

Multiple locations, each with its own opening hours and dedicated service catalog. Service duration drives the slot grid and the chair-dashboard live status.

Security & Access Control

Three groups live under the Salon Management application category:

  • Salon Manager — full access to branches, services, stylists and appointments.
  • Salon Stylist — read access to the catalog, full access only to appointments assigned to them.
  • Salon Booking (Portal) — portal users manage their own bookings.

Access tokens are scoped per appointment: every customer receives a private, signed URL to view and cancel their booking even without a portal account.
